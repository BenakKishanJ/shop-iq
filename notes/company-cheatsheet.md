## 1. The One-Liner

> IgniteIQ is a **forward-deployed AI engineering firm** — they embed with a client's team, co-design the solution, and ship **agentic AI into production**, replacing SaaS and automating operations.

**Pitch (3 bullets):**
- ⚡ **Speed:** "weeks, not quarters"
- 🔑 **Ownership:** "your IP, your stack" (no lock-in)
- 📊 **Outcomes:** "measured against ROI, not hours" (not billed per engineer-hour)

## 2. Engagement Model

**Pod:** architect + fractional CTO + AI-augmented engineers, embedded *inside* the client's team. Decisions made in the room, not handed over.

**4 impact areas:** embedded forward-deployed engineers · agentic solutions (agents that plan/act/learn) · SaaS replacement & custom build · change management, governance & trust.

**3 phases:**
1. **Discover** — frame problem, quantify opportunity, align on measurable outcomes
2. **Co-Design** — prototype on the client's *real* data & workflows
3. **Deliver & Adopt** — ship to production, drive adoption, hand over the keys
